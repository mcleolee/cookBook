常见问题

数据保存在哪里？

饭本默认将菜谱、计划、冰箱和图片保存在当前设备。“我的 → 数据 → iCloud 同步”入口始终可见，默认关闭、需会员权益，由你主动确认后开启，可在使用同一 Apple 账号的 iPhone、iPad 和 Mac 间同步。请定期使用“我的 → 导出数据”保存饭本完整备份；恢复入口为“我的 → 恢复饭本备份”。

开启同步时如何选择？

可以合并两边数据、用本机替换 iCloud，或用 iCloud 替换本机。覆盖操作会影响其他已开启同步的设备，请先核对预览并保存备份。关闭同步保留本机与云端数据。做菜进度、计时器及设备显示偏好不参与同步。

为什么没有收到通知？

请在“我的 → 提醒设置”开启对应提醒，并在系统设置中允许饭本通知。iOS/iPadOS 26 支持系统计时与实时活动，需要闹钟权限；iOS/iPadOS 17–25 与 Mac 使用普通计时和通知，不能保证静音、专注模式或后台状态下响铃。饭本不会向苹果时钟添加计时器。

网页导入失败怎么办？

确认网址以 http:// 或 https:// 开头，并检查网络连接。不同网站格式可能不同；失败后可返回手动编辑页面录入菜谱。

如何从料理笔记导入？

打开“我的 → 导入数据”，选择料理笔记导出的 ZIP 或 Markdown。推荐 ZIP；饭本只会处理你选择导入的菜谱，并限制文件、图片和解压大小。

购物篮如何合计食材？

计划页切换到“购物篮”，可以按菜查看或合计同名食材。克与千克、毫升与升会自动换算；“少许、适量”等原文会保留并提示自行调整。

如何使用相机或照片？

编辑菜谱时点击封面选择照片；支持拍摄的 iPhone/iPad 可使用相机。Mac 可通过系统文件选择器选择图片。若之前拒绝权限，请前往系统设置 → 饭本重新开启。

如何反馈问题？

请邮件联系 1772133019@qq.com，附上“我的 → 关于饭本”中的 App 版本与 Build、系统版本、设备型号、复现步骤和必要的截图。请不要发送菜谱隐私内容。

会员与免费版

免费版可保存15道正式且未删除的菜谱（草稿及最近删除不计入）、10条冰箱食材，今天及未来合计安排4道菜；历史计划不占额度。下厨房导入、官方精选、备份恢复与导出免费。其他网站与料理笔记批量导入、冰箱食材匹配及 iCloud 同步需要会员。会员到期后保留数据和编辑能力，超额限制新增。

订阅、试用与取消

中国大陆正常价格为月付人民币9.90元、季度24.80元、年度88.80元，最终以 Apple 购买确认页为准。符合资格的 Apple 账号可在同一订阅组享受一次14天免费试用，之后按所选周期自动续费。首版不提供五折优惠。请至少提前24小时在系统“设置 → Apple 账号 → 订阅 → 饭本”取消续订；卸载 App 不会取消订阅。重新安装后可从“我的 → 饭本会员 → 恢复购买”恢复有效权益。

CookBook Support

For help, email 1772133019@qq.com with your app version/build, device, system version and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not include passwords or private recipes.

Storage, backups and sync

Personal content is local by default. Create a full backup from profile/settings → Export Data and restore it using Restore CookBook Backup. Optional iCloud sync requires active membership and the same Apple account on supported devices. Review merge and overwrite previews before enabling sync, and keep a backup. Turning sync off retains local and cloud content. Cooking progress, timers and display preferences are not synced.

Free tier and membership

The free tier supports 15 saved recipes excluding drafts and Recently Deleted, 10 fridge entries and 4 planned dishes across today/future dates; past plans do not count. Nutrition browsing, featured browsing, Xiachufang imports and backup/restore/export are free. Membership removes these limits and enables other compatible website imports, PlateNote bulk imports, fridge matching and optional iCloud sync. Expiry retains content while limiting additions and pausing membership sync.

Subscriptions and cancellation

Monthly, quarterly and annual plans renew automatically at the price shown by Apple for your region. Eligible Apple accounts can receive one two-week free trial per subscription group. To avoid renewal, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends in device Settings → Apple account → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription. Use Restore Purchases on the membership screen to restore active entitlements. Apple Standard EULA applies.

Notifications and permissions

Enable reminders in CookBook and grant the corresponding system permission. iOS/iPadOS 26 offers supported system cooking alarms and Live Activities; older systems and Mac use ordinary timers and notifications. System settings affect background alerts. Camera/photo selection, Calendar and Reminders are used only for their associated actions.

Imports, featured content and nutrition

Web import support depends on source formatting and access restrictions; review imports and edit manually when needed. Featured content needs internet for the initial download and can be cached. Disable auto-caching in Featured settings if desired. The nutrition table includes offline source-attributed reference data; it is not personalized medical advice.

Deletion and compatibility

Recently Deleted retains items until permanent deletion. Back up before restoring or clearing data, particularly with sync enabled. iPhone/iPad require iOS/iPadOS 17 or later; the Mac Catalyst build requires macOS 15 or later. Platform and regional availability follow the App Store listing.
