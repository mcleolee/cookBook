常见问题

数据保存在哪里？

饭本默认将菜谱、计划、冰箱和图片保存在当前设备。“我的 → 数据 → iCloud 同步”入口始终可见，默认关闭、需会员权益，由你主动确认后开启，可在使用同一 Apple 账号的 iPhone、iPad 和 Mac 间同步。请定期使用“我的 → 导出数据”保存饭本完整备份；恢复入口为“我的 → 恢复饭本备份”。

开启同步时如何选择？

可以合并两边数据、用本机替换 iCloud，或用 iCloud 替换本机。覆盖操作会影响其他已开启同步的设备，请先核对预览并保存备份。关闭同步保留本机与云端数据。做菜进度、计时器及设备显示偏好不参与同步。

为什么没有收到通知？

请在“我的 → 提醒设置”开启对应提醒，并在系统设置中允许饭本通知。iOS/iPadOS 26 支持系统计时与实时活动，需要闹钟权限；iOS/iPadOS 17–25 与 Mac 使用普通计时和通知，不能保证静音、专注模式或后台状态下响铃。饭本不会向苹果时钟添加计时器。

网页导入失败怎么办？

确认网址以 http:// 或 https:// 开头，并检查网络连接。不同网站格式可能不同；失败后可返回手动编辑页面录入菜谱。

如何从料理笔记导入？

打开“我的 → 导入数据”，选择料理笔记导出的 ZIP 或 Markdown。推荐 ZIP；饭本只会处理你选择导入的菜谱，并限制文件、图片和解压大小。

购物篮如何合计食材？

计划页切换到“购物篮”，可以按菜查看或合计同名食材。克与千克、毫升与升会自动换算；“少许、适量”等原文会保留并提示自行调整。

如何使用相机或照片？

编辑菜谱时点击封面选择照片；支持拍摄的 iPhone/iPad 可使用相机。Mac 可通过系统文件选择器选择图片。若之前拒绝权限，请前往系统设置 → 饭本重新开启。

如何反馈问题？

请邮件联系 raven42lee@outlook.com，附上“我的 → 关于饭本”中的 App 版本与 Build、系统版本、设备型号、复现步骤和必要的截图。请不要发送菜谱隐私内容。

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

CookBook stores recipes, meal plans, fridge entries and images on your current device by default. Profile → Data → iCloud Sync is always visible, off by default and requires membership. You can choose to enable it to sync between supported iPhone, iPad and Mac devices using the same Apple account. Regularly save a full backup from Profile → Export Data; restore it from Profile → Restore CookBook Backup.

Which sync option should I choose?

You can merge both sides, replace iCloud with this device, or replace this device with iCloud. Overwrites can affect other synced devices, so review the preview and keep a backup first. Turning sync off retains local and cloud data. Cooking progress, timers and device display preferences are not synced.

Why am I not receiving notifications?

Enable the relevant reminders in Profile → Reminder settings and allow CookBook notifications in system settings. iOS / iPadOS 26 supports system alarms and Live Activities and requires alarm permission. iOS / iPadOS 17–25 and Mac use ordinary timers and notifications; alerts are not guaranteed in silent mode, Focus or the background. CookBook does not add timers to Apple’s Clock app.

What if a webpage import fails?

Check that the URL begins with http:// or https:// and that you have a network connection. Website formats vary. If importing fails, return to manual editing and enter the recipe yourself.

How do I import from PlateNote?

Open Profile → Import Data and select a ZIP or Markdown export from PlateNote. ZIP is recommended. CookBook processes only the recipes you select and applies limits to files, images and extracted size.

How are shopping ingredients combined?

Open Shopping List on the Plan tab to view items by dish or combine ingredients with the same name. Grams / kilograms and milliliters / liters are converted automatically. Text such as “a little” or “to taste” is retained for you to adjust.

How do I use the camera or photos?

Tap the cover when editing a recipe to choose an image. Supported iPhone and iPad devices can use the camera; Mac uses the system file picker. If you previously denied permission, re-enable it in system settings → CookBook.

How do I report a problem?

Email raven42lee@outlook.com with your app version and build from Profile → About CookBook, system version, device model, steps to reproduce and relevant screenshots. Please avoid including private recipe content.

AI ingredient entry and calorie tools

AI supports text and voice ingredient organization, photo and receipt recognition, webpage recipe organization and photo calorie estimates, with individual features becoming available gradually by app version. Review names, amounts and estimates before confirming; unknown quantities may remain empty. Speech is transcribed on-device or by Apple speech services. Recordings are not retained or sent to CookBook AI; text is sent only when you confirm generation.

All online AI features share 15 lifetime free requests and 200 member requests per Beijing calendar month. Speech transcription and local ingredient nutrition calculations do not use AI credits. AI requires sign-in; check the app for available features and remaining credits.

Membership and the free tier

The free tier supports 15 saved, non-deleted recipes, excluding drafts and Recently Deleted; 20 fridge entries; and 4 dishes in total across today and future dates. Past plans do not count. Xiachufang imports, featured content, backup, restore and export are free. Other website imports, PlateNote bulk imports, fridge ingredient matching and iCloud sync require membership. After membership expires, data and editing access remain, while additions beyond free limits are restricted.

Subscriptions, trials and cancellation

Mainland China reference prices are CNY 9.90 monthly, CNY 24.80 quarterly and CNY 88.80 annually. Final prices are shown in Apple’s purchase confirmation. Eligible Apple accounts may receive one 14-day free trial per subscription group, followed by automatic renewal at the selected plan’s regular price. No half-price introductory offer is provided. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal in system Settings → Apple account → Subscriptions → CookBook. Uninstalling does not cancel the subscription. After reinstalling, restore active purchases from Profile → CookBook Membership → Restore Purchases.
