饭本隐私政策 / CookBook Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-09-25

English

概述

饭本（CookBook）由个人开发者维护，是菜谱管理和餐食计划工具。当前版本不要求注册饭本账号，不提供 AI 服务，不集成广告或跨应用追踪 SDK。个人内容默认保存在设备本地；iCloud 同步由你主动开启。

本地内容与照片

菜谱、步骤、笔记、计划、冰箱食材、购物篮、便当、烹饪进度及图片默认保存在饭本的设备存储中。照片首次保存时移除定位等无关元数据，保留供重新裁切使用的高质量图片与裁切参数，不自动写入系统相册。开发者不通过自建服务器接收你的个人菜谱、照片或笔记。

可选 iCloud 同步

在“我的 → 数据 → iCloud 同步”主动开启后，支持同步的菜谱与图片、标签、计划、购物篮、冰箱、便当及内容设置会上传至当前 Apple 账号的 CloudKit 私人数据库，供你自己的兼容设备使用。处理受 Apple 的 iCloud 服务条款和隐私政策约束。做菜进度、计时器、通知权限、提醒偏好、显示布局和首次引导进度不参与同步。退出或更换账号会暂停同步，新账号需重新确认开启。同步默认关闭，需有效会员权益。

Apple 会员订阅

购买、试用、付款及续订由 Apple 处理，饭本通过 StoreKit 验证交易与有效期以判断权益，不接收银行卡信息。会员状态不写入菜谱备份。会员到期不会删除已有内容；超出免费额度时限制新增，并暂停会员同步。恢复订阅后请主动重新开启同步。

精选、营养更新与网络服务

饭本通过 HTTPS 从阿里云 OSS 获取公开精选目录、原创菜谱、贴士和图片。默认在 App 启动或回到前台时按刷新间隔检查精选并预取内容；你可以在“我的 → 精选设置”关闭自动缓存或清除缓存。手动打开和刷新精选仍可能访问网络。食材营养表内置可离线读取的数据；进入相关营养页面时可能检查公开更新清单并下载更新包。

为传输内容，网络服务商会处理 IP 地址、请求时间、资源路径和响应状态等必要网络信息。当前未启用该内容存储空间的访问日志转存，开发者查看云服务提供的请求量和流量汇总以维护服务，不建立个人访问档案，不用于跨应用追踪或广告定向。以后如改变日志留存或分析方式，将相应更新政策及商店隐私说明。这些请求不附加饭本个人账号或广告标识，也不上传你的私人菜谱、计划或冰箱内容。营养资料的出处和版本可在 App 中查看。

网页与文件导入

仅在你主动输入或粘贴网址进行导入时，饭本才直接访问对应网站，网站可能处理该请求的 IP 等网络信息。若导入文件引用网络图片，饭本可能访问该图片地址。App 不上传你的浏览历史。ZIP、Markdown 等文件由你主动选择，导入临时文件在完成、取消或失败后清理。导入与分享后，请同时考虑源网站和接收方的数据处理规则。

系统权限

  • 相机与照片选择：用于你选择的菜谱图片，不自动扫描相册。
  • 通知、闹钟与实时活动：用于你启用的计时和食材提醒；iOS/iPadOS 26 的系统计时使用对应系统能力。
  • 日历完全访问：仅在你主动导出餐食计划时请求，用于创建及更新饭本导出的事件，避免重复。
  • 提醒事项访问：仅在主动导出购物篮时请求，用于创建、更新清单及已买状态。

饭本不会把你其他日历事件或提醒事项上传给开发者。导出后的内容可能随系统账号同步；关闭饭本同步或卸载 App 不会删除已导出的事件与清单。拒绝某项权限只影响相关功能。

备份、分享与删除

你可以主动导出完整备份或分享菜谱。导出文件由你选择的存储位置或接收方保管，请妥善保存。菜谱和便当的“最近删除”长期保留内容，需手动永久删除。可在“我的”使用“清空全部数据”清除支持的本地内容及相关通知；启用同步时，恢复或清空可能传播到其他同步设备，操作前会提示确认。关闭同步保留本机与云端数据；卸载也不会自动删除 iCloud 数据。同步安全副本和冲突记录保存在本机，需单独删除。

English — Privacy Policy

Overview and local content

CookBook is maintained by an individual developer. This release does not require a CookBook account, does not offer AI services, and has no advertising or cross-app tracking SDK. Personal recipes, notes, meal plans, fridge entries, shopping lists, bento content, cooking progress and images are stored on your device by default. Photos are saved without location and other unnecessary metadata; a high-quality image and crop settings are retained for later editing. The app does not automatically save photos to your photo library. The developer does not receive your private recipes or photos through a custom server.

Optional iCloud and Apple subscriptions

iCloud sync is off by default and requires active membership. If you enable it in profile/settings → Data → iCloud Sync, supported personal content and settings are stored in your Apple account's private CloudKit database for your compatible devices, subject to Apple's terms and privacy policy. Cooking progress, timers, permissions, reminders preferences, display layout and onboarding progress are not synced. Changing or signing out of the account pauses sync and requires confirmation for a new account.

Apple handles purchases, payment, trials and renewal. CookBook uses StoreKit to verify transactions and entitlement expiry; it does not receive payment card details. Entitlements are not included in recipe backups. Membership expiry retains existing content, restricts additions beyond free limits and pauses membership sync. After restoring membership, enable sync again if desired.

Featured content, nutrition updates and network requests

Public featured recipes, tips and images are downloaded over HTTPS from Alibaba Cloud OSS and cached locally. By default, the app checks and prefetches featured content on launch or return to the foreground, subject to refresh intervals. Disable automatic caching or clear the cache in Featured settings; manual browsing and refreshing may still make requests. The nutrition table includes offline data; relevant screens may check a public update catalog and download updates.

To deliver content, the hosting provider processes necessary network information such as IP address, request time, resource path and response status. Access-log storage for this content bucket is currently disabled. The developer views aggregate request and traffic metrics to maintain the service, without building individual browsing profiles, cross-app tracking or targeted advertising. Changes to log retention or analysis will be reflected in this policy and the store privacy disclosures. Requests do not include a proprietary account or advertising identifier, or upload your private recipes, plans or fridge entries. Nutrition sources and versions are shown in the app.

Imports, permissions and exports

Web imports contact the address you choose directly, and referenced remote images may also be fetched. Those websites may process network information such as IP addresses. CookBook does not upload your browsing history. You choose files for import; temporary import files are removed on completion, cancellation or failure.

Camera and photo selection are used for images you choose. Notifications, alarms and Live Activities support enabled timers and ingredient reminders. Calendar full access and Reminders access are requested when you choose an export, to create and update CookBook entries without duplicates. Other calendar or reminder content is not uploaded to the developer. Exported entries may sync through the system account and are not removed by uninstalling CookBook. Declining a permission affects the associated feature.

Your controls

Backups and shared recipes are stored with the destinations or recipients you choose. Recently Deleted retains recipes and bento content until you permanently delete them. Clear All Data removes supported local content and associated notifications; when sync is enabled, restore and clearing operations may propagate to other devices after confirmation. Disabling sync keeps local and cloud data. Uninstalling does not automatically delete iCloud content. Local sync safety copies and conflict records must be removed separately.

Contact and changes

Contact 1772133019@qq.com about privacy. Do not send passwords, payment information or unnecessary private content. Policy changes will be published here with an updated effective date.
