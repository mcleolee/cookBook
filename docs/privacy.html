饭本隐私政策 / CookBook Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-10-29

AI统一同意与必要数据

仅在你主动提交AI任务时，饭本才会通过HTTPS发送完成该任务所需的数据。登录本身不会上传你的个人菜谱库、照片库或整个冰箱。

文字入库和网页整理会发送你提交的提示词、文字或网页正文；图片识别会发送本次所选图片。语音任务只向饭本AI发送确认的转写文字；转写优先在设备上进行，不支持时由Apple语音服务处理，录音不发送给饭本AI。

生成菜谱会发送你填写的菜名、食材、份数、时间、菜系和生成条件，以及实际使用的饮食偏好、过敏、忌口与补充备注；使用冰箱配菜时还会发送相关可用库存的名称、数量、单位和剩余比例。

AI热量计算会发送当前菜谱的名称、食材、步骤和份数；选择照片估算时还会发送本次所选照片。照片不会被自动保存为菜谱封面。本地食材数据库计算不向AI发送菜谱。

这些内容经饭本腾讯云上海服务器交由中国北京地域的阿里云百炼Qwen模型处理。临时任务内容的保存、供应商处理及删除规则见下文；饭本不授权将你的输入输出用于广告或模型训练。

AI可能出错或遗漏，请核对食材、过敏、步骤、数量和热量后再使用。拒绝或撤回同意不影响本地功能；已派发的处理无法保证撤回。本机当前账号同意后，各AI功能无需重复确认；说明更新或撤回后需要重新同意。

概述

饭本（CookBook）由个人开发者维护，是菜谱管理和餐食计划工具。本地功能不要求注册；可选AI图片、文字、网页整理、食材标准化、菜谱生成和照片热量需要Apple登录及明确许可。不集成广告或跨应用追踪SDK。个人内容默认保存在本地；iCloud由你主动开启。

AI上传许可、饮食偏好与地区

在你阅读完整AI说明、勾选同意并主动点击处理前，饭本不发送该次AI内容。所选图片、文字、可访问网页正文或食材会经HTTPS发送至饭本腾讯云服务器及阿里云百炼。AI生成菜谱还会使用当前选择的忌口、过敏、喜好和补充备注。你可以拒绝，本地功能不受影响；用途或政策实质变化时会重新确认。不要提交与任务无关的个人资料。

支持全球连接的新版本允许海外用户尝试AI服务，实际可用性受所在网络影响，尚未完成海外手机登录、上传和AI生成的完整实测。旧版本可能仍限制中国大陆App Store。数据经中国上海的饭本腾讯云服务器发送至当前阿里云百炼服务；这不是海外独立部署。Apple验证的独立沙盒使用独立账户、数据和预算，不授予正式额度。AI可能错误或遗漏，需核对食材、过敏、步骤和热量。

个人次数与服务保护限制

免费累计15次及会员每北京时间自然月200次是个人额度；正式服务另有全体用户合计每日200次的成本保护上限和并发限制。达到服务限制时会提示，未成功生成有效结果不扣个人次数，不会自动新建收费请求。开发和沙盒不占正式每日保护量。

原图与派生图片

用于显示、精选内容和AI传输的派生图片可能在设备或服务端按用途缩小、转为兼容色彩空间及压缩；不保证固定压缩比例。不会因传输优化批量替换已有原图。完整备份和个人同步保留饭本已保存的完整构图素材及裁切参数；首次保存继续移除定位等无关元数据。

本地内容与照片

菜谱、步骤、笔记、计划、冰箱食材、购物篮、便当、烹饪进度及图片默认保存在饭本的设备存储中。照片首次保存时移除定位等无关元数据，保留供重新裁切使用的高质量图片与裁切参数，不自动写入系统相册。普通菜谱照片和笔记不上传至饭本服务器；你主动提交的 AI 识别图片按下方说明处理。

可选 AI、语音与热量处理

文字或语音录入冰箱时，停止后整理的文字会发送至饭本服务器和阿里云百炼。语音实时转写由设备支持时优先在设备端完成，否则由Apple语音服务转写；录音不发给饭本AI、不保存录音，只有你确认生成时发送文字。结果需核对并确认后才写入冰箱。

你可主动把可访问网页正文交给AI整理为菜谱；饭本不在服务器任意抓取URL，不绕过登录或验证码，保留来源并由你检查步骤和用量。菜谱热量计算使用你选择的营养记录、克重和份数，缺失营养值不会按零处理。照片热量只处理你在菜谱热量入口选取的本次照片，用后删除，不作为封面；估算需你确认，照片、遮挡和隐藏油糖会造成较大误差。

可选 AI 图片入库与账户

AI需要使用支持当前服务范围的App版本。你点击识别后，饭本将去除无关照片元数据并通过 HTTPS 上传所选图片至腾讯云上海服务器，再发送至阿里云百炼进行食材识别。请勿上传身份证件、付款信息或无关个人信息。AI 结果可能有误，须由你编辑确认后才保存至冰箱。未知数量可以留空，不自动推断到期日。

成功处理后饭本删除输入图片；失败任务的加密输入可为重试保留至创建后最多24小时，过期清除。识别结果草稿在服务器和设备独立加密临时区最多保留24小时，供断线恢复，不进入普通备份或iCloud。确认保存的食材成为本地业务数据。该期限仅适用于饭本，百炼的必要记录及备份规则另见第三方处理章节。

AI需要使用Apple登录。为验证账号及防止重复领取，饭本保存由Apple稳定身份计算的HMAC标识，不长期保存Apple明文subject、姓名或登录邮箱。HMAC是可关联的假名标识，不是匿名数据。账号有效期间，身份及最小合计额度计数用于提供服务；删除账号后仅保留HMAC、身份提供方、必要时间及免费已用合计、本月会员已用合计，最长730天用于反滥用。到期清理未重新启用账号的该标记；账号重建时仅继承尚在保留期内的额度，不保存历史菜谱、照片或识别正文。免费累计15次、有效会员每北京时间自然月200次。

任务正文、图片和完整结果加密保存在独立临时区，最多至任务创建后24小时；成功处理后输入正文和图片立即移除，失败输入可在该期限内用于你主动重试。临时内容不进入例行、部署前或导出备份。运行明细用于安全、排错及核对，最多180天；到期删除账号关联的任务编号、调用明细及关联账本，仅保留不含个人标识的月度统计及仍有效账号的免费已用合计数。合计数不包含单次输入、时间或任务编号。账号和有效交易状态保留至账号删除或服务所需期限结束。

在AI账号页可退出、撤回本机上传许可或删除AI账户。退出不等于删除；撤回后不提交新任务，已派发的处理无法保证撤回。删除会撤销饭本会话和Apple授权、移除账号、任务内容、调用明细和关联交易记录，本地菜谱及冰箱不变。仅保留上述最长730天的最小反滥用标记及合计额度。服务器加密备份最多保留28天，到期移除；旧备份恢复前必须应用最新删除记录，避免已删除账户复活。Apple自身交易记录由Apple处理；删除饭本AI账户不会取消订阅，请到Apple订阅管理取消。

可选 iCloud 同步

在“我的 → 数据 → iCloud 同步”主动开启后，支持同步的菜谱与图片、标签、计划、购物篮、冰箱、便当及内容设置会上传至当前 Apple 账号的 CloudKit 私人数据库，供你自己的兼容设备使用。处理受 Apple 的 iCloud 服务条款和隐私政策约束。做菜进度、计时器、通知权限、提醒偏好、显示布局和首次引导进度不参与同步。退出或更换账号会暂停同步，新账号需重新确认开启。同步默认关闭，需有效会员权益。

Apple 会员订阅

购买、试用、付款及续订由 Apple 处理，饭本通过 StoreKit 验证交易与有效期以判断权益，不接收银行卡信息。会员状态不写入菜谱备份。会员到期不会删除已有内容；超出免费额度时限制新增，并暂停会员同步。恢复订阅后请主动重新开启同步。

精选、营养更新与网络服务

饭本通过 HTTPS 从阿里云 OSS 获取公开精选目录、菜谱、贴士和图片，其中包括附来源及许可说明的第三方内容。默认在 App 启动或回到前台时按刷新间隔检查精选并预取内容；你可以在“我的 → 精选设置”关闭自动缓存或清除缓存。手动打开和刷新精选仍可能访问网络。食材营养表内置可离线读取的数据；进入相关营养页面时可能检查公开更新清单并下载更新包。

为传输内容，网络服务商会处理 IP 地址、请求时间、资源路径和响应状态等必要网络信息。开发者查看请求量和流量汇总以维护服务；必要访问日志可能私有保留最多30天，用于安全排查和内容下载统计，不建立个人访问档案，不用于跨应用追踪或广告定向。日志不代表独立阅读人数。处理方式发生变化时更新政策和商店隐私说明。这些请求不附加饭本个人账号或广告标识，也不上传你的私人菜谱、计划或冰箱内容。营养资料的出处和版本可在 App 中查看。

网页与文件导入

仅在你主动输入或粘贴网址进行导入时，饭本才直接访问对应网站，网站可能处理该请求的 IP 等网络信息。若导入文件引用网络图片，饭本可能访问该图片地址。App 不上传你的浏览历史。ZIP、Markdown 等文件由你主动选择，导入临时文件在完成、取消或失败后清理。导入与分享后，请同时考虑源网站和接收方的数据处理规则。

  • 相机与照片选择：用于你选择的菜谱图片，不自动扫描相册。
  • 通知、闹钟与实时活动：用于你启用的计时和食材提醒；iOS/iPadOS 26 的系统计时使用对应系统能力。

备份、分享与删除

你可以主动导出完整备份或分享菜谱。导出文件由你选择的存储位置或接收方保管，请妥善保存。菜谱和便当的“最近删除”长期保留内容，需手动永久删除。可在“我的”使用“清空全部数据”清除支持的本地内容及相关通知；启用同步时，恢复或清空可能传播到其他同步设备，操作前会提示确认。关闭同步保留本机与云端数据；卸载也不会自动删除 iCloud 数据。同步安全副本和冲突记录保存在本机，需单独删除。

功能建议与问题反馈

联系开发者允许自愿提交功能或问题建议，无需AI登录。普通提案保存类别、标题、说明和HMAC化安装标识；昵称及邮箱为选填，仅审核通过的标题、说明和昵称公开，邮箱加密且不公开。私人举报不公开，限开发者核查，最多保存180天；屏蔽/封禁标记最长365天。普通提案保留至删除或不再需要展示；可邮件申请删除。匿名额度事件仅保留当周，不含正文和邮箱。屏蔽会隐藏同一来源的公开提案和关联计划；此安装标识不是跨App追踪标识。

连接检查与私人诊断

你可以主动检查服务接口连接，不上传AI正文或图片，也不调用模型或扣次数。错误诊断仅在内存中保留版本、设备型号、系统、语言、可取得的商店地区、时间、错误分类和请求编号；不会自动上传。复制、分享或邮件发送前可查看内容。邮件由你选择的邮件服务处理；请勿加入账号凭据、照片或私人菜谱。服务器另记录必要请求编号、状态和耗时用于故障排查，沿用现有运行记录保留规则。

第三方数据处理与保护

饭本的AI处理方为通义云启（杭州）信息技术有限公司提供的阿里云百炼服务，当前使用中国北京地域的Qwen模型接口；网络和存储服务还涉及腾讯云与阿里云OSS。我们仅向受服务协议约束的处理方发送完成任务所需的数据，不授权广告、出售或模型训练用途，并要求按处理指令、限制访问、加密和最短必要期限提供与本政策及Apple要求相符的保护。百炼服务协议第6节限制未经授权的使用和披露、允许按指令删除或更改，并说明必要留存及备份延迟删除。供应商可能依法保存调用数据，具体期限随适用条款与法定义务，不等于饭本的24小时期限；我们不声称供应商零留存。你可以联系饭本隐私邮箱，我们会协助向处理方转达访问、更正或删除请求。

备份与删除申请

服务器日常、部署前和导出备份均排除临时正文、图片、完整结果、登录会话及Apple刷新令牌，服务器备份最长28天；对历史部署快照采用相同去敏与轮换规则。备份仅用于受控恢复，并先应用最新身份与删除记录。你可通过AI账号页删除账号，其他个人数据/提案及邮件诊断可向隐私邮箱申请访问、更正或删除；我们核实必要身份后按适用法律期限处理，通常30天内答复，需延长时说明原因。邮件诊断不自动进入公开提案，处理完成后由开发者在30天内清理不再必要的邮件内容；依法必须保存的最小记录会说明目的和期限。

CookBook Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-29

Unified AI consent and necessary data

Only when you submit an AI task does CookBook send the data needed for that task over HTTPS. Signing in does not upload your personal recipe library, photo library or entire fridge.

Text import and web recipe extraction send your submitted prompts, text or web page content. Image recognition sends the selected image. Speech tasks send only the confirmed transcript to CookBook AI. Transcription takes place on your device when supported, otherwise through Apple speech services. Recordings are not sent to CookBook AI.

Recipe generation sends your recipe name, ingredients, servings, time, cuisine and instructions, together with the dietary preferences, allergies, restrictions and notes used for generation. Fridge suggestions also send relevant available inventory names, amounts, units and remaining proportions.

AI calorie estimation sends the current recipe name, ingredients, steps and servings. Photo estimation also sends the selected photo. Photos are not automatically saved as recipe covers. Local nutrition database calculations do not send recipes to AI.

This content passes through CookBook on Tencent Cloud in Shanghai to Qwen models in Alibaba Cloud Model Studio in China (Beijing). See below for temporary-content retention, provider processing and deletion. CookBook does not authorize advertising or model-training use of your inputs or outputs.

AI may make mistakes or miss information. Check ingredients, allergies, steps, quantities and calories before using results. Declining or withdrawing consent does not affect local features. Processing already dispatched may not be retractable. Once this account consents on this device, AI features do not ask again unless the notice changes or consent is withdrawn.

Overview

CookBook is maintained by an individual developer. Local features do not require registration. Optional AI image and text processing, web recipe extraction, ingredient normalization, recipe generation and photo calorie estimates require Sign in with Apple and explicit permission. CookBook has no advertising or cross-app tracking SDK. Personal content is local by default; iCloud is opt-in.

AI consent, dietary preferences and availability

No AI task content is sent before you read the full AI notice, check the agreement box and choose to process it. Selected images, text, accessible web page text or ingredients are transmitted over HTTPS to the CookBook service on Tencent Cloud and Alibaba Cloud Bailian. Recipe generation also uses the selected dietary restrictions, allergies, preferences and notes. Declining does not affect local features. Material policy or purpose changes require renewed permission. Do not include unrelated personal information.

The new version with global connectivity lets users outside mainland China try AI. Availability depends on their network, and overseas mobile sign-in, upload and AI workflows have not been tested end to end. Older versions may still restrict AI to the mainland China App Store. Data passes through CookBook on Tencent Cloud in Shanghai, China, to the current Alibaba Cloud Model Studio service; this is not a separate overseas deployment. The Apple-verified sandbox has separate accounts, data and budgets and grants no production credits. Review ingredients, allergens, steps and calories: AI can make mistakes or omit information.

Personal credits and service protection

The ten lifetime free credits and 200 credits per Beijing calendar month for members are personal allowances. The production service separately applies a shared 200-dispatch daily cost-protection limit and concurrency limits. Service limits are explained; unsuccessful tasks do not spend personal credits and no new paid task is silently retried. Development and sandbox traffic do not use the production daily budget.

Original and derivative images

Derivative images for display, featured content and AI transfer may be resized, color-managed and compressed on the device or server for their purpose; no fixed compression ratio is guaranteed. Transfer optimization does not batch-replace saved originals. Complete backups and personal sync retain the full-frame assets already saved by CookBook and crop parameters. Initial photo storage continues to remove location and unrelated metadata.

Local content and photos

Recipes, steps, notes, meal plans, fridge ingredients, shopping lists, bento content, cooking progress and images are stored on your device by default. When a photo is first saved, location and other unnecessary metadata are removed. A high-quality image and crop settings are retained for future editing, without automatically saving to your system photo library. Regular recipe photos and notes are not uploaded to CookBook servers. Images you explicitly submit for AI recognition are processed as described below.

Optional AI, speech and calorie processing

For text or voice ingredient entry, the text prepared after you finish is sent to CookBook servers and Alibaba Cloud Bailian. Live speech transcription is performed on-device when supported, otherwise by Apple speech services. Recordings are not sent to CookBook AI or retained; text is sent only when you confirm generation. Results are saved to the fridge only after you review and confirm them.

You may choose to submit accessible webpage text for AI recipe organization. CookBook does not fetch arbitrary URLs from its servers or bypass logins or CAPTCHAs. The source is retained for you to review steps and amounts. Recipe calorie calculations use the nutrition records, gram weights and servings you select; missing nutrition values are not treated as zero. Photo calorie estimates use only the photo you select for that request; it is deleted after use and is not used as a cover. Estimates require confirmation and may have substantial errors due to portions, occlusion or hidden oil and sugar.

Optional AI image entry and accounts

AI requires an app version supporting the current service availability. When you choose recognition, CookBook removes unrelated photo metadata and uploads the selected image over HTTPS to its Tencent Cloud server in Shanghai, China, then to Alibaba Cloud Model Studio for ingredient recognition. Do not upload identity documents, payment details or unrelated personal information. Review and edit results before saving to the fridge. Unknown quantities may remain empty; expiry dates are not automatically inferred.

CookBook removes input images after successful processing. Encrypted input for failed tasks may remain for retry until at most 24 hours after creation, then expires. Recognition drafts remain in separate encrypted server/device temporary storage for at most 24 hours for recovery, outside normal backups and iCloud. Confirmed ingredients become local data. These limits apply to CookBook; provider records and backups follow the third-party processing section.

AI requires Sign in with Apple. CookBook stores an HMAC derived from the stable Apple identity for account verification and preventing repeated free claims, without long-term storage of the raw Apple subject, name or sign-in email. This is a linkable pseudonymous identifier, not anonymous data. Identity and minimal aggregate credit counts support active accounts. After deletion, only the HMAC, identity provider, necessary timestamps, lifetime free-use total and current member-month total remain for up to 730 days for abuse prevention. Expired markers for accounts not reactivated are removed; recreated accounts inherit only retained counts, without old recipes, photos or recognition content. AI includes 15 lifetime free uses or 200 active-member uses per Beijing calendar month.

Task text, images and full results are encrypted in a separate temporary area until at most 24 hours after creation. Input text and images are removed after successful processing; failed input can support a user-initiated retry within that period. Temporary content is excluded from routine, deployment and exported backups. Operational details support security, troubleshooting and reconciliation for at most 180 days. Account-linked task identifiers, call details and related ledger entries are then deleted, leaving monthly statistics without personal identifiers and aggregate lifetime free-use counts for active accounts. Counts contain no individual inputs, timestamps or task references. Accounts and applicable transaction state are retained until account deletion or the end of their necessary service purpose.

The AI account screen offers sign-out, withdrawal of local upload consent and account deletion. Signing out does not delete an account. Withdrawing consent stops new submissions but may not retract dispatched processing. Deletion revokes CookBook sessions and Apple authorization and removes the account, task content, call details and associated transaction records, while keeping local recipes and fridge data. Only the minimal abuse-prevention marker and aggregate credit counts described above remain for up to 730 days. Encrypted server backups are kept for at most 28 days; restores must apply the latest deletion records to prevent deleted accounts from returning. Apple controls its own transaction records. Deleting the AI account does not cancel subscriptions; cancel them in Apple subscription settings.

Optional iCloud sync

If you enable Profile → Data → iCloud Sync, supported recipes and images, tags, meal plans, shopping lists, fridge entries, bento content and content settings are uploaded to your current Apple account’s private CloudKit database for your compatible devices. Processing is subject to Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy policy. Cooking progress, timers, notification permissions, reminder preferences, display layout and onboarding progress are not synced. Signing out or changing accounts pauses sync; a new account requires confirmation to enable it again. Sync is off by default and requires active membership.

Apple membership subscriptions

Apple handles purchases, trials, payment and renewals. CookBook uses StoreKit to verify transactions and validity periods to determine membership access; it does not receive payment card details. Membership status is not included in recipe backups. Expiry does not delete existing content; additions beyond free limits are restricted and membership sync pauses. After restoring your subscription, enable sync again if you want to use it.

Featured content, nutrition updates and network services

CookBook downloads public featured catalogs, recipes, tips and images over HTTPS from Alibaba Cloud OSS, including attributed third-party content with license information. By default it checks and prefetches featured content on launch or return to the foreground, subject to refresh intervals. Disable automatic caching or clear the cache in Featured settings; manual browsing and refresh may still make requests. Nutrition includes offline data; relevant pages may check public update catalogs and download updates.

To deliver content, hosting providers process necessary network information such as IP address, request time, resource path and response status. The developer uses aggregate request and traffic metrics to maintain the service. Necessary access logs may be retained privately for up to 30 days for security troubleshooting and content download statistics, without individual browsing profiles, cross-app tracking or ad targeting. Downloads do not represent unique readers. Requests do not include a proprietary account or advertising identifier and do not upload private recipes, plans or fridge content. Nutrition sources and versions are shown in the app. Changes are reflected in this policy and store disclosures.

Web and file imports

CookBook contacts a website directly only when you enter or paste its URL for import. The website may process network information such as your IP address. If an imported file references remote images, CookBook may request those image URLs. The app does not upload browsing history. You choose ZIP, Markdown and other import files; temporary import files are cleaned up after completion, cancellation or failure. When importing or sharing, also consider the source website’s and recipient’s data practices.

Backups, sharing and deletion

You can choose to export a full backup or share recipes. Exported files are stored with the destination or recipient you choose; keep them safe. Recently Deleted retains recipes and bento content until you permanently delete them manually. Clear All Data in Profile removes supported local content and associated notifications. With sync enabled, restoring or clearing may propagate to other synced devices after confirmation. Turning sync off retains both local and cloud content, and uninstalling does not automatically delete iCloud data. Local sync safety copies and conflict records must be removed separately.
